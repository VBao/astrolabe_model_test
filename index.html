Four Astrolabes.

الأسطرلاب

The brief: “3D model this Islamic astrolabe.” Four AI models answered. Pick one to view, or open it full screen.

Keys: 1-4 switch versions, ←/→ previous and next, Esc close.

Heads-up: this page is open from file://. Three of the four instruments use ES modules and will not render in the embedded viewer. Serve the folder instead: python3 -m http.server 8000 and open http://localhost:8000/. (The DS 4.1 F version works either way.)

DS 4.1 F

offline

A single-file build with a dark gallery presentation: polished brass rings, a slider for every ring, an auto-rotate button and a live FPS readout.

Test notes

10 turns, seven of them mechanical fixes. The pole sat on the wrong axis, rings turned in the wrong plane, floated off the frame, and one build turned the pole instead of the rings. The per-ring sliders and auto-rotate were added once the joints held.

GLM 5.3 Flash

needs internet

A full-bleed historic reconstruction with dense Arabic engraving and bilingual ring controls. three.js is pulled from the unpkg CDN, so it needs a connection.

Test notes

4 turns. The first pass slid the rings across the plate instead of turning them around the main pole; later fixes seated the whole instrument back in its (rotatable) plate and connected both ends of the pole to it. Densest engraving of the four.

Fable 5.1

offline

An editorial split layout: four rings and a globe sharing one axle, patina-toned brass with teal controls, plus a “Reset pose” action.

Test notes

1 turn, no corrections. The rings tilt on the shared axle as the brief asked: one slider each, plus a reset pose. Lightest engraving of the four; the finish is in the materials.

Opus 5.5

offline

Verdigris-inlaid rings in a throne-crowned frame, five rotating parts, three ring arrangements (Flat / Fanned / Crossed) and a reference-photo overlay.

Test notes

1 turn, no corrections. Hinge, axle and five rotating parts came out right on the first pass; it adds posed ring arrangements (Flat / Fanned / Crossed) and a reference-photo overlay. Closest to the photo’s composition.
